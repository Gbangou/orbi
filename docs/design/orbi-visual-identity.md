# Orbi Visual Identity

## Benchmark Position

Orbi should feel as operationally serious as Uber, as direct and energetic as
Bolt, as locally adaptable as Yango, and as human as Lyft, without copying any
competitor's brand.

## Palette

- Obsidian `#071311`: primary trust color, used for text, dark surfaces and
  high-confidence actions.
- Emerald `#00B894`: rider mobility signal, realtime confidence, success and
  map motion.
- Sun Gold `#F2A900`: driver energy, earnings, premium service and money flows.
- Signal Blue `#246BFE`: system state, route intelligence and neutral
  operational signals.
- Soft Surface `#F6F8F7`: calm app background that keeps dense mobility UI
  readable in daylight.

## Vehicle Assets

Rider service cards use generated premium bitmap assets per service tier:

- `moto-standard`: miniature Burkina-style urban underbone motorcycle with
  emerald accents, exposed to riders simply as **Moto**.
- `car-standard`: clean white compact sedan.
- `car-comfort`: black executive sedan.
- `car-xl`: white high-capacity SUV/minivan.

Source images are kept in `docs/design/vehicle-assets/source`. App-ready
transparent PNGs live in `apps/rider-app/assets/vehicles`.

## Product Rules

- Vehicle imagery should be photorealistic or premium 3D, never toy-like.
- Service tiers must be visually distinct at a glance.
- Rider surfaces prefer emerald accents; driver surfaces prefer sun-gold accents.
- Maps should use clean light basemaps with emerald route emphasis for field
  readability.
